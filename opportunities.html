Good to know

Do you sell these courses?

No. The courses belong to the providers named on each card. You join and pay on their website. We only check the courses and list the good ones.

Can a provider pay to skip the review?

No. Every course goes through the same review, whether the provider pays for a featured spot or not.

What if a course is not as promised?

Write to me through Ask Me in the Student Zone. We look into every complaint and remove courses that let students down.

How long does a review take?

Usually 7 to 10 working days after we receive demo or trial access.
