How these jobs are checked

Every job title here is one that Management Students in India are really hired for. A title stays only if it appears in India's National Classification of Occupations or in current job posts and official recruitment notifications. Government roles always depend on the latest notification, so read it before you apply.

There are no salary figures on purpose: pay depends on your skill, the company and your experience. New roles and briefs are added regularly.
