Create your LinkedIn in 15 minutes

Follow these steps on your phone or laptop.

  1. Sign up freeTap the blue button below. Use your personal email (not your college one), so you keep your profile after you graduate.
  2. Add a clear photoFace the camera, plain background, good light, a smile. Formal or smart-casual clothes.
  3. Add your educationYour university, programme and specialisation, with start and end years.
  4. Set your location and "Open to work"Choose internships or jobs and the roles you want.
  5. Come back hereWe'll write your headline, About section, experience lines and skills for you to paste in.
  6. Connect with 50 peopleClassmates, faculty, seniors and alumni. A bigger network means recruiters see you more.
