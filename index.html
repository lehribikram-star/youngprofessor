About

I am an Associate Professor at Chandigarh University, where I teach the flagship dual-specialisation MBA and the M.A. in Economics. My subject areas are Marketing, Organisational Behaviour, Entrepreneurship and Finance, and I hold a Ph.D. in Finance from Punjabi University, Patiala.

My teaching is built around outcome-based curricula, guided student research and classroom methods that keep students engaged. Alongside teaching, I have served as NSS Programme Officer, as Superintendent for examinations, and as an admission counsellor who went on to lead the university's admissions team.

I care most about mentoring: helping students find their direction, prepare for exams like UGC NET, and grow into confident professionals. This site is where I share my academic journey, research and what I am working on now.
