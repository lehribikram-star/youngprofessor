About me

Teaching that builds future-ready leaders.

“I chose academia not just as a profession, but as a platform to shape thinking and develop future-ready leaders.”

I am an Associate Professor at the University School of Business, Chandigarh University, with a Ph.D. in Finance from Punjabi University, Patiala and more than a decade in higher education. I have qualified UGC-NET four times across Commerce and Management, and I teach Marketing, Finance, Entrepreneurship and Organisational Behaviour on the flagship MBA and M.A. Economics programmes.

My focus is on preparing students to think critically, act ethically and handle real-world business challenges with confidence. Beyond teaching, I serve as NSS Programme Officer and Deputy Superintendent (Examination), and I have led admissions counselling. What drives me is student transformation: seeing learners grow into confident, capable professionals.

My research looks at corporate governance, corporate social responsibility and sustainability in emerging economies, published with Springer, CRC Press, IGI Global and in Scopus and UGC-CARE listed journals.
