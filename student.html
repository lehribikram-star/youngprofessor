UGC-NET Corner

Crack NET with a plan

I have qualified UGC-NET four times in Commerce and Management and have coached NET aspirants for years. Start here.

The exam at a glance

Paper 1 (common for all)50 questions · 100 marks
Paper 2 (your subject)100 questions · 200 marks
Total150 questions · 300 marks
Duration3 hours, one sitting
Marking+2 per correct · no negative
ModeComputer-based test
Always confirm the latest notice on the official site: ugcnet.nta.ac.in ↗

Paper 1 units

  • Teaching aptitude
  • Research aptitude
  • Comprehension
  • Communication
  • Mathematical reasoning
  • Logical reasoning
  • Data interpretation
  • ICT
  • People, development & environment
  • Higher education system
  1. Attempt every questionThere is no negative marking, so never leave a question blank.
  2. Start with past papersSolve previous years' papers first to see which units repeat most.
  3. Make Paper 1 your scoring paperTeaching, research and data interpretation are learnable. Practise them daily.
  4. Revise in short cyclesRevisit each unit every week in 30-minute sessions rather than one long reading.
  5. Take full mock testsPractise the full 3 hours at once so your speed and focus are ready on the day.
  6. Stuck on a topic?Ask me. Chances are other students are stuck on it too.

Wellbeing Corner

It is okay to pause

Exams, placements and deadlines can feel heavy. These tools help you calm down in a few minutes, and you never have to handle things alone.

Box breathing · 1 minute

Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, breathe out for 4, hold for 4. Follow the circle.

Ready
4 rounds · about 1 minute

5-4-3-2-1 grounding

  1. Notice 5 things you can see
  2. 4 things you can touch
  3. 3 things you can hear
  4. 2 things you can smell
  5. 1 thing you can taste
